Find The Audience That Actually PAYS

Your market has 10 audiences inside. Your job: pick ONE that has weekly pain, talks loud in ONE place, and already buys. If they say "any free alternative?" RUN.

SIGNAL 1 • CAN PAY ★
They say "worth every penny" or buy $19/$39/$99 on Gumroad. If "free alternative?" = RUN
SIGNAL 2 • CONCENTRATED
3 places: FB 20k + Reddit 50k + LinkedIn. If scattered, you'll burn ads.
SIGNAL 3 • VOCAL
Complain DAILY with long posts. Silent audience = no sales.
SIGNAL 4 • JOB
Check profiles: employees? freelancers $$$? Not students/job seekers.
SIGNAL 5 • RECOMMENDATION
"What tool do you recommend?" = strongest buying intent.
